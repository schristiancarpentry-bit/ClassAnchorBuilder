Components

Drag a component onto the board. Drag any unlocked component to reposition it, or drag its corner/end handle to stretch it longer or shorter. Move it close to a matching connection point on another component and it snaps into place.

Lock / Unlock

Once a component is snapped onto a connection point, tap the padlock on it to lock it there — locked pieces can't be dragged or resized. Tap the padlock again to unlock and reposition. Only locked pieces count toward the level/plumb check below.

Level & Plumb Check

Drag the spirit level onto any placed component. It reads TRUE (bubble centred) once that component is locked and its chain of locked connections traces all the way back to a Ground / Floor Datum — or OUT if anything along the way is unlocked or missing.
