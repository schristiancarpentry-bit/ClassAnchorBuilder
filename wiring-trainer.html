Voltmeter

Drag the meter body to place it, then drag the red and black probe tips onto any terminal to test the voltage there — just like a real meter, it reads the difference between the two probe points. Tap the dial to switch it on/off.

2-Way Switches, the 20A DP Switch and the Fused Spur can be tapped on the board to flip them — try flipping a switch with both probes on the lamp's terminals to watch the reading change between 0V and 230V.
