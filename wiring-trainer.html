ℹ How to wire

Press and drag from a terminal, then release: over open board it drops a route point (press-drag-release again to keep going), over another terminal it connects and finishes. Esc or right-click cancels. Pick a colour again for the next wire, even the same one.

Circuit Test

Flip the power switch on the Consumer Unit to test the wiring. A correct path lights up and flows through to whatever it reaches; a wrong-coloured or crossed connection sparks and blows right at the mistake. A terminal with nothing wired to it just stays dark — safe, but not working. The DP switch, fused spur and 2-way switch are tappable too — a 2-way switch always routes to L1 or L2 (never "off"), so a real two-way lighting circuit only lights up when both switches happen to align.
